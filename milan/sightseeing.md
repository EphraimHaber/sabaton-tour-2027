## History and military

### HIS-01 - Museo Nazionale Scienza e Tecnologia + Enrico Toti submarine

- category: history-military
- why: A real Cold War attack submarine (S 506 Enrico Toti) you can go inside, plus aircraft, locomotives and the Conte Biancamano liner section. The closest thing in Milan to a Sabaton setlist.
- area: Via San Vittore 21, centre-west (M2 Sant'Ambrogio)
- time_needed: 2.5-3 h including the submarine tour
- price_pp: Museum NIS 46 (EUR 13). Visitors aged 3-26 pay the concession rate NIS 29 (EUR 8) with ID. Toti interior tour is an add-on of NIS 36 (EUR 10), so NIS 82 (EUR 23) full or NIS 64 (EUR 18) concession.
- booking: The submarine tour **cannot be bought separately**: buy "Museum admission + guided tour of the Toti" as one ticket. The tour is **in Italian only**, small groups, sells out - buy ahead online. Museum entry itself: "booking recommended".
- hours_sun_18_apr: 09:30-18:30 (last entry 1 h before closing)
- hours_mon_19_apr: **CLOSED** (closed on working Mondays)
- hours_tue_20_apr: 09:30-17:00
- official_url: https://www.museoscienza.org/en
- source_url: https://www.museoscienza.org/en/visiting/tickets and https://www.museoscienza.org/en/visiting/information
- status: `confirmed` (hours pattern "from 15 September 2026"; Toti tour slots for April 2027 not on sale yet / not checked)

## Where to stay by vibe

**Assago / Milanofiori (by the arena):** walk back after the show and the easiest place to leave bags on show day, but it is an office-and-mall district with nothing to do at night; every outing is a 15-25 minute M2 ride. **Navigli (M2 Porta Genova):** the best fit for this group - canal bars and aperitivo on the doorstep, and the same M2 line runs direct to the arena, so bags can be fetched after the show without changing trains. **Centrale:** least charming, most practical for anything involving a train or an airport coach - Malpensa trains and coaches, the Verona/Como/Bergamo trains, the Shoah Memorial and Rock'n'Roll Milano are all there, and M2 runs direct to Assago in about 24 minutes. For the red-eye plans (MIL-01/02/07) Centrale or Assago wins; for the stay-over plans (MIL-03/04/11) Navigli wins. Design Week ends on Sunday 18 Apr, so compare Sunday-night rates across all three before deciding.
