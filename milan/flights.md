## How to read the prices

- `NIS total (3)` is the figure the source displayed for 3 adults. `NIS pp` = total / 3, rounded.
- Primary source: **Google Flights with `curr=ILS`** (price shown directly in ILS, no conversion by me).
- Second source: **Kayak Israel (il.kayak.com)**, which also displays ILS.
- Kayak ILS figures are consistently **7-9% lower** than Google ILS for the same flights (see "Cross-check"). Google matches the airlines' own prices (Wizz EUR fares, El Al's and Israir's own quotes); Kayak's lower rows are third-party agency prices. Budget with Google.
- Wizz Air sells in EUR. Google showed the same Wizz fares as EUR 225 pp (W4 6434, 18 Apr) and EUR 200 pp (W4 6431, 20 Apr), i.e. Google's implied rate is **1 EUR = 3.582 NIS** (2418 / 675). Ground-transport amounts below are converted at **3.58** and marked as converted.
- USD items (Israir trolley fee) are converted at Google's implied **1 USD = 3.254 NIS** (2418 / 743).
- Base fares exclude a cabin trolley on Wizz and Israir. See "Bags".

Verification vocabulary:

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `verified` | This exact flight number, time and price was read from a live Google Flights result (3 adults, ILS) |
| `verified-sum` | Each leg verified separately as a one-way; the total is my sum of two one-way prices (no single ticket) |
| `kayak-only` | Times and price read live on Kayak Israel; not shown on Google Flights; flight number NOT seen |
| `schedule-only` | Flight seen, price not |
| `unverified` | Not seen on a live page; from secondary source or estimate |
