## Findings that change the earlier README numbers

- There is still no nonstop TLV-DUB flight on these dates; the shortest connection is 7h40-7h45.
- The README's "NIS 1,424 pp via FRA" was a EUR 401 quote converted at 3.55. The same Lufthansa round trip shown directly in ILS is **NIS 1,560 pp** (Lufthansa's own price on the Google booking page: NIS 4,681 = $1,439 for 3). Kayak shows it at $461 pp = NIS 1,497 pp. Budget 1,500-1,580. Fact-check 18 Nov 2026: the gap is the fare, not the exchange rate - the Bank of Israel representative rate for 17 Nov 2026 is 1 EUR = 3.5683 NIS (the README's 3.55 is 0.5% lower), so EUR 401 is NIS 1,431 today while NIS 1,560 is EUR 437; Trip.com shows round trips with the same outbound from NIS 1,580 pp.
- No group penalty: the Lufthansa fare is 1,560 pp with 1, 2 or 3 adults.
- Lufthansa's fare here is Economy Light, which includes an 8 kg cabin trolley. Lufthansa also sells a newer "Economy Basic" (personal item only) on some routes - check the fare name at checkout.
- Leaving Monday costs 133 pp more than Tuesday on Lufthansa, but Monday is cheaper than Tuesday on Air France/KLM.

## How this was collected

Scripts are in `scripts/` (Python + Playwright driving the installed Chrome; they load ordinary Google Flights URLs with `curr=ILS`, 3 adults, and read the result data the page itself fetches). Raw captures are in `data/raw/`, Kayak page text and screenshots in `data/crosscheck/`. `scripts/build_md.py` regenerates this file and `data/itineraries.json` from the raw captures. Google rate-limited the first sweep (its "unusual traffic" page); the sweep was stopped and resumed later at a slower pace, so the planned sweep of about 55 other European transfer airports was not run - Kayak's self-transfer results were used to cover that ground instead.
