# Fact-check - Dublin flight research (Sabaton, 3Arena, Wed 7 Apr 2027)

Checked on 18 Nov 2026, 00:46-01:13 Israel time (UTC+2), about half an hour after the author's own captures. Files checked: `flights.md`, `sources.md`, `data/itineraries.json`. All prices are NIS for 3 adults unless marked pp (per person).

Verdicts: **confirmed** (independently reproduced), **corrected** (claim was inaccurate or incomplete; the files have been edited), **wrong** (claim was false; edited), **could-not-verify** (reason given).

## How to read the evidence

- **Google re-read**: every Google Flights booking link in `flights.md` was re-opened in a fresh browser session (3 adults, `curr=ILS`). This is the author's own source, so it shows the price is reproducible today, not that it is right. The first attempt at the FR117 link returned Google's generic error page three times (00:54-00:56) and loaded normally on a retry at 00:57.
- **Trip.com**: a third source, in ILS, 3 adults. It supplies flight numbers and times independently of Google and Kayak. Caveat: Trip.com's response tagged its booking channel as "AntiCrawler", so its prices are indicative only; where they agree with Google within 1-3% I treat that as support, where they differ I say so. Its round-trip list shows a "from" price per outbound without naming the return flight, so a round-trip figure from Trip.com is a lower bound for that outbound, not a price for the exact pair.
- **FlightAware**: shows what each flight number operates *now* (Nov 2026 winter schedule). It confirms that the number exists on the claimed route; it cannot confirm April 2027 times, which still differ from winter times by 5-55 minutes.
- **Airline APIs**: Ryanair's timetable and fare APIs and Wizz Air's timetable API are the airlines' own data. No other airline site could be queried (see Remaining unknowns).
- Layovers, durations and per-person figures were recomputed from `data/itineraries.json` with explicit UTC offsets (Israel, Greece, Romania +3; Germany, France, Netherlands, Italy +2; Ireland, UK +1). No arithmetic error was found in any itinerary table.
- All 31 Google links in `flights.md` were decoded (the `tfs` parameter): every one encodes the right airports, dates, flight numbers, 3 passengers and trip type.
