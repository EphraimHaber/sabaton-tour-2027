## Findings that change the earlier README numbers

- There is still no nonstop TLV-DUB flight on these dates; the shortest connection is 7h40-7h45.
- The README's "NIS 1,424 pp via FRA" was a EUR 401 quote converted at 3.55. The same Lufthansa round trip shown directly in ILS is **NIS 1,560 pp** (Lufthansa's own price on the Google booking page: NIS 4,681 = $1,439 for 3). Kayak shows it at $461 pp = NIS 1,497 pp. Budget 1,500-1,560.
- No group penalty: the Lufthansa fare is 1,560 pp with 1, 2 or 3 adults.
- Lufthansa's fare here is Economy Light, which includes an 8 kg cabin trolley. Lufthansa also sells a newer "Economy Basic" (personal item only) on some routes - check the fare name at checkout.
- Leaving Monday costs 133 pp more than Tuesday on Lufthansa, but Monday is cheaper than Tuesday on Air France/KLM.
