### Things found that change the plan

1. **Show time conflict.** The ticket seller bol.pt lists doors 17:30 and show 19:00. The MEO Arena's own page lists the session as 20h30 and a 90-minute show. Re-check both before fixing the Thursday plan, and before choosing LIS-07 (23:40 flight the same night).
2. **Megadeth, Testament and Black Label Society play Campo Pequeno on Tue 13 Apr 2027 at 19:00** (₪178-232 / €50-65, Time Out listing; last stop of Megadeth's farewell European tour). Only reachable with a Monday arrival (LIS-02, LIS-14) or the Tuesday 09:15 TAP arrival (LIS-05). The Tuesday Aegean (22:45) and El Al (20:55) arrivals miss it. The only kosher restaurant in Lisbon is on the same square.
3. **Chabad is in Cascais, not Lisbon.** Chabad Portugal's own site places the centre in Cascais (about 30 km west). It is not walkable from any Lisbon hotel. A "Lisbon Chabad House" at Rua do Açúcar 101 appears on one Israeli travel site only and could not be confirmed.
4. **The Lisbon community (CIL) Shabbat dinner must be booked and paid by Thu 15 Apr 18:00**, which is concert day. The form opens the Monday before and closes when full.
5. **Football:** Sporting are at home to Marítimo in round 30, listed for the weekend of 18 Apr 2027. The exact day and kick-off are not yet scheduled. Benfica are away (Nacional, Madeira).
6. **Passover starts Wed 21 Apr 2027 evening.** The week of 12-16 Apr is the pre-Passover week; confirm that the kosher restaurant and shops keep normal hours.

### SHB-01 Shaaré Tikvah Synagogue (Comunidade Israelita de Lisboa)
- **Why:** The only confirmed Shabbat services inside Lisbon; Friday-night community dinner on site.
- **Area:** Rua Alexandre Herculano 59, Rato / Marquês de Pombal.
- **Time needed:** Fri 19:30 service; Sat 09:00 service.
- **Price pp:** Services free. Registration is mandatory.
- **Booking:** Yes. Non-members and tourists fill in the security form **by Fri 11:00** (send it days earlier; the form gets no reply). No luggage, suitcases or shopping bags allowed in the building. ID may be requested.

## Jewish heritage

- **Price pp:** Free.

### JEW-03 Tomar Synagogue (Abraham Zacuto Luso-Hebrew Museum) - day trip
- **Why:** Portugal's oldest preserved synagogue (mid-15th century). Tomar is also the Knights Templar headquarters (Convento de Cristo and Templar castle), so it doubles as a military-history day.
- **Area:** Rua Dr. Joaquim Jacinto 73, Tomar.
- **Time needed:** Full day.
- **Price pp:** price not confirmed for the synagogue (unofficial: free, user review), for the Convento de Cristo (unofficial: reseller listings only) and for the train.
- **Booking:** No for the synagogue.
- **Shabbat:** No.
- **Official:** https://www.cm-tomar.pt/visitar/o-que-visitar/sinagoga
- **Price/hours source:** Tomar council flyer, undated: Apr-Sep Tue-Sun 10:00-13:00 and 14:00-18:00, closed Mondays - https://cm-tomar.pt/images/CMT/visitar/documentos/mapas/flyer_sinagoga.pdf (seen in search summary; the council site reset the direct fetch)
- **Notes:** JNS describes Tomar as "a little over an hour and a half" from Lisbon. Do not go on a Monday.

## Where to stay by vibe

With the Saturday-night flight the group needs to be on foot from Friday sunset, so the Shabbat base decides everything: stay in **Rato / Marquês de Pombal / upper Avenida da Liberdade**, within about 1 km of Rua Alexandre Herculano 59, with the kosher shop on Rua do Salitre close by and Príncipe Real, Parque Eduardo VII and the Gulbenkian garden in walking range. It is a business-and-embassy area, calmer at night than the old town, on the blue and yellow metro lines; the arena is a metro ride with one change to the red line. **Baixa / Chiado** is the better base for bars, ginjinha and Bairro Alto and is still about 1.5-2 km on foot from the synagogue, which is a workable but long Shabbat walk. **Parque das Nações** is modern, flat and five minutes from the arena and the airport, but it is roughly 8 km from the synagogue with nothing old around it; it only makes sense for the Thursday-night dash (LIS-07), or as a one-night stay on Thursday for a group willing to move hotels on Friday morning. Distances here are approximate and were not measured.
