## Friday verdict

**A return that lands by 12:00 Friday exists, but only one, and only as a self-transfer on separate tickets.** Nothing protected on a single ticket makes it.

|---|---|---|---|

| Aegean A3723 + A3924 on one ticket | 12:50 | 50 min late | FAIL |
| Any departure from Lisbon on Friday morning | 15:25 at the earliest | 3h25 late | FAIL |

**Caveat from the fact-check:** the one compliant option needs the group to leave the arena about 22:00. Sabaton's set on this tour runs 2h05-2h10 and is likely to end 22:35-22:45; Aegean closes the gate in Lisbon 30 minutes before departure (23:10). Without leaving early there is no Friday-compliant return at all.

Evidence that nothing else exists:
- **Friday-morning departures cannot work at all.** The first departure from Lisbon is 05:05. A nonstop takes 5h10 and Israel is 2 hours ahead, so even a nonstop at 05:05 would land at 12:15. The earliest real arrival is 15:25 (Lufthansa via Frankfurt). 16 itineraries leaving 00:00-09:59 Friday were checked on Google. Arkia's Friday nonstop IZ254 leaves 10:15 and lands 17:30.
- **Thursday-night departures after the show:** nonstop departures at 22:00 or later were queried on Google from Lisbon to 33 hub airports (2 of the 33 queries, Krakow and Thessaloniki, were blocked and not retried). Three late departures exist that would be reachable after a 22:00 finish: Aegean to Athens 23:40, Air Serbia JU565 to Belgrade 23:00 and TAP TP1938 to Porto 23:45 (the last seen in the Lisbon - Tel Aviv results). Onward from Belgrade the earliest TLV arrival is 14:35; from Porto 16:00. Only Athens works.
- **The two nonstops on Thursday night leave too early.** TAP TP1603 departs 20:50 (during the show) and El Al LY376 departs 22:15 (the show ends 22:00 at the earliest). Both would land Friday 04:15 / 05:25.
- **Athens to TLV on Friday morning** has four flights after the 05:35 arrival: Arkia 09:40, Wizz 09:55, El Al LY542 10:30 (lands 12:30), Aegean A3924 10:50 (lands 12:50). Sky Express GQ720 leaves at 03:30, before the Lisbon flight lands. A detour via Larnaca lands 12:45.
- **Porto, Madrid, Faro, Seville:** none is reachable after the show in time for anything earlier. Madrid's El Al night flight LY398 leaves 22:50 Thursday.
- Kayak's own search for Lisbon departures after 22:30 Thursday, sorted by arrival, returned the same answer: earliest 12:00 (Aegean + Wizz), then 12:45, then 12:50.

If the self-transfer is missed in Athens, the same-morning fallbacks land at 12:30 (El Al), 12:50 (Aegean) and 14:15 (Israir 6H562) - after the 12:00 cutoff but 5 to 6.5 hours before sunset.
