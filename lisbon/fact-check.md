# Lisbon flights - independent fact-check

Checked 18 Nov 2026, 00:45 to 01:15 Israel time. 3 adults, economy, prices in NIS exactly as each site displayed them (no conversion). Nothing booked, no personal details sent.

Verdict words: **confirmed** / **corrected** (flights.md was changed) / **could-not-verify** / **wrong**.

## Headline findings

1. **LIS-07 is not realistic as written.** The venue lists the session at 20:30, the ticket seller at 19:00 (doors 17:30). On the 2025 leg of this tour the orchestra opened about 90 min before Sabaton and Sabaton played 2h05-2h10. Either reading puts the end at about 22:35-22:45, not 22:00. Aegean closes the gate at Lisbon at least 30 min before departure (23:10). It only works by leaving the show around 22:00.
2. **The Arkia TLV-LIS nonstop does exist** and is on sale for April 2027 - on Mondays and Fridays only. flights.md looked for it on Tue, Wed and Sun. It does not create a new compliant return (Fri lands 17:30; Mon lands Tue 03:15).
3. **Lisbon Saturday nightfall is about 20:56, not 20:40.** LIS-01's 00:40 departure is still compliant. The rejected 21:20 and 21:30 Saturday departures are even tighter than stated.
4. **The codeshare rule is one-directional.** El Al numbers on TAP-operated flights cost up to 2.5x. But TAP's number on El Al's LY376 is 10-24% *cheaper* than El Al's own number.
5. **Lufthansa (LIS-08) went up about 8%** between the author's check and mine (₪6,081 -> ₪6,589 for 3).
6. All other flight numbers, times, durations, layovers and arithmetic checked out. Google prices reproduced within ₪1-10 except Lufthansa.
